Candlestick

Create animations and interactive media in this beginner-oriented tool, all in your browser!

Candlestick is a fork of Wick Editor, a discontinued multimedia creation tool still loved by millions worldwide. It fixes crucial bugs and adds long-requested features, making Candlestick the definitive version of Wick Editor.
